About

I spent 9+ years in finance and project accounting in Indonesia — starting as a Project Accountant and eventually leading financial oversight across multiple construction sites. Along the way, I saw firsthand how much a single unreliable number can cost: a delayed decision, a missed budget, a project review that goes sideways. That's what drove me to go back to school.

I'm now finishing my MSc in Data Analytics for Business at KEDGE Business School in Bordeaux, building the technical skills — SQL, Python, Power BI, machine learning — to do the work I always wanted to do but couldn't: turn raw financial data into something a business can actually act on. The numbers I care about are the ones that prevent a bad decision, not just summarise it.

I completed a Business Intelligence Analyst internship at Amplitude Laser Group, building Looker Studio dashboards and optimising BigQuery pipelines for an after-sales team that was still running everything on spreadsheets. I'm now doing a People Strategy Business Analyst internship at Doctolib, building a Metabase-based People Data Hub that gives HR teams one source of truth on attrition, demographics, and workforce composition. I work in English daily, still building my French, and Indonesian at home.
